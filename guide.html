Documentation & Referencing Rules

Learn how to use the automated generator tool and review IIE Harvard Anglia (2025) formatting standards.

// 2. IIE Harvard Anglia Reference Rules

1. Book (Print & eBook)

In-Text Format:

Paraphrased: (Surname, Year) or Surname (Year)

Direct Quote: (Surname, Year, p. X) or Surname (Year, p. X)

*Note: Use p. for single pages and pp. for page ranges. For 4+ authors, use et al. in-text.
Reference List Format:
Author Surname, Initials. Year. Title of book. Edition (if not 1st). Place of publication: Publisher.

Example: Hüther, G., 2006. The compassionate brain: How empathy creates intelligence. Boston: Trumpeter.

2. Journal Article (Print / Online Database)

In-Text Format:

Paraphrased: (Barker, 2009)

Direct Quote: (Barker, 2009, p. 7)

Reference List Format:
Author Surname, Initials. Year. Title of article. Name of Journal, Volume(Issue), pp. page range. Available at: <URL/DOI> [Accessed Date].

Example: Barker, R., 2009. A qualitative thematic analysis of Facebook profiles. Journal of Qualitative Research, [e-journal] 42(1), pp.7–14. <https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1745691612442904> [Accessed 22 July 2023].

3. Website / Webpage

In-Text Format:

Paraphrased: (World Health Organization, 2022)

Direct Quote: (World Health Organization, 2022)

Reference List Format:
Author Surname, Initials. Year. Title of webpage. [online] Available at: <URL> [Accessed Date].

Example: Foundation for Economic Education, 2014. England's Whetstone named FEE's first Blinking Lights Award recipient. [online] Available at: <https://fee.org/resources/englands-whetstone-named-fees-first-blinking-lights-award-recipient/> [Accessed 16 July 2014].

4. Blog Article

In-Text Format:

Paraphrased: (Geezer, 2009)

Direct Quote: (Geezer, 2009)

Reference List Format:
Author Surname, Initials. Year. Title of individual blog entry. Blog Title, [blog] Posting Day Month. Available at: <URL> [Accessed Date].

Example: Geezer, D., 2009. Conservationists are not making themselves heard. Guardian.co.uk Science blog, [blog] 18 June. Available at: <https://www.theguardian.com/science/blog/2009/jun/18/conservation-extinction-open-ground> [Accessed 23 June 2009].

5. Online Video (YouTube / TEDx)

In-Text Format:

Paraphrased: (Defra, 2007)

Direct Quote: (Defra, 2007: 02:15) (Include timestamp for video quotes)

Reference List Format:
Author / Channel Name. Year. Title of video. [video online] Available at: <URL> [Accessed Date].

Example: Defra, 2007. Sustainable development: the bigger picture. [video online] Available at: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=keZmg56ahdM> [Accessed 23 June 2012].

6. Student Material (Module Outlines / Lecture Notes / Learn Portal)

In-Text Format:

Paraphrased: (The Independent Institute of Education, 2025)

Direct Quote: (The Independent Institute of Education, 2025, p. 12)

Reference List Format:
Author / Institution. Year. Title of module outline / lecture unit. [Module Guide / Lecture Notes]. Place: Publisher / Portal.

Example: The Independent Institute of Education (IIE), 2025. Academic Literacy (ACLI5111) Module Outline. [Module guide]. Johannesburg: The Independent Institute of Education.
